# Deco AI

Teach it a decoration style and it decorates your level the way creators do: blocks, slopes, spikes, orbs, pads, portals and more, with colours included. Everything runs on your own computer: no API key, no internet.

## Teach it a style

**Learn:** decorate an example and select it **together with its deco**, then press **Learn**. You can teach it several things at once:

- **A block:** outlines along edges, glow, corner caps and small details are each recognised by where they sit. A side you didn't decorate copies its opposite side.
- **A spike, slope, orb, pad, portal or any other object:** the deco around it is copied exactly, relative to the object, and follows the object's rotation, flips and size.
- **A whole decorated section:** select a finished part of a level (yours or someone else's) and press Learn. It finds the repeating patterns and keeps the most common variants.

The **colour channels** your example uses are saved with the style, including copy-colour links.

**Palette (quick):** select loose deco pieces and press **Palette**. It sorts them by shape into outlines, details and large pieces.

## Decorate

Select what you want decorated (blocks, spikes, orbs, slopes, anything) and press **Deco!** One undo removes the whole run.

Colours come with the deco. A channel your level doesn't use yet is set up as learned. If your level already uses that channel number for something else, the deco is moved to a **free channel**, so your existing colours are never changed.

## Styles menu

**Styles** lists every style you've taught it, each with a picture of its example. From there you can pick the style **in use**, rename or delete styles, or start a **new** one. Learn always adds to the style in use.

## What it will never do

- Place spikes, orbs, pads, portals, coins, triggers or anything with collision. Only decoration objects are used.
- Put block deco in the open air where you play, or cover orbs, pads and portals with it.
- Treat the border between selected and unselected blocks as an edge. Only real, visible edges count.
- Stack duplicates when you press Deco! again.
- Recolour channels your level already uses.
- Copy your example's group IDs, unless you turn on *Keep group IDs*.

## Settings

Turn outlines, corners, edge details, underside details, interior details and large pieces on or off individually. **Detail density** sets how busy the details get.
